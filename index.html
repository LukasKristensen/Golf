Session report

Golfparken Aalborg Sessions

Seven sessions on a six-hole loop, played by a rotating group of six. Lower is better throughout. Par is 3 per hole, 18 for the loop, and every hole is capped at 9 (triple par). Session 1 was played before the cap, so its scores are capped here to match. The latest session was played on 1 November 2026.

Observations

What stands out

Results

Mikkel ends Laurits's streak

Mikkel won session 7 with 34, seven strokes better than their previous best of 41 and the third lowest round so far after Tobias's 28 and Laurits's 29. It ended a run of four straight wins for Laurits, who finished third on 39 behind Daniel's 38.

Laurits still sets the pace

Laurits has the best average at 37.3, 5.5 strokes ahead of anyone else, and leads every regular head to head: 5-0 with a tie against Lukas, 4-2 against Daniel and 3-1 with a tie against Mikkel. All three losses came in session 1 (to Daniel) and session 7 (to Mikkel and Daniel).

A new order behind Laurits

Session 7 moved Mikkel from fourth to second on average. Mikkel (42.8), Daniel (43.6) and Lukas (44.1) are now 1.3 strokes apart. Lukas leads Mikkel 3-2 with a tie, Mikkel leads Daniel 3-2 with a tie, and Daniel and Lukas are level at 3-3 with a tie.

Three close finishes

Session 1 (Daniel by 3), session 4 (Laurits by 2) and session 7 (Mikkel by 4) were decided by fewer than 8 strokes. The other four winners won by 8 to 15.

Scores came back down in session 7

The field averaged 43.3, 40.5 and 38.8 in the first three sessions, then 43.8, 44.8 and 45.0. Session 7 broke that run at 39.0, the second best field average after session 3.

Session 7 at a glance

Four players: Mikkel 34, Daniel 38, Laurits 39 and Lukas 45. Mikkel and Laurits both reached the greens in 19 strokes; the difference was putting, where Mikkel took 15 and Laurits 20, the most in any counted round.

Players

Lukas and Daniel have played every session

Both were at all seven. Lukas has scored 43 to 45 in five of them, with a best of 38 in session 3 and 51 in session 6. Daniel ranges from 38 to 48; the 38 in session 7 is their best, three better than the capped 41 that won session 1.

Daniel is the steadiest

Daniel's totals vary least (spread of 3.0 strokes), then Lukas (3.5). Mikkel's spread rose to 4.5 with the 34, and Laurits is at 4.6, mostly because of 44 in session 1; Laurits has not scored above 39 since.

Each player's best hole

Hole 5 suits Laurits best at 4.7, the lowest any regular averages on a hole. Daniel is best on hole 3 (6.0), Mikkel on hole 2 (6.3) and Lukas on hole 4 (6.9).

Who fades and who finishes

Laurits gets better over the round, from 19.3 on holes 1 to 3 to 18.0 on holes 4 to 6. Daniel goes the other way, from 21.3 to 22.3, and has taken the maximum 9 on hole 6 in five of seven rounds. Mikkel had taken 9 on hole 4 in all five earlier rounds, then made a 4 there in session 7.

Holes

Hole 1 is the hardest

Hole 1 plays hardest at 7.7, ahead of hole 6 (7.5) and hole 4 (7.4). Hole 5 is the easiest at 6.2. About three holes in ten end on the maximum of 9 (52 of 174).

The course rating does not match this group

The club rates hole 2 hardest (SI 1) and hole 4 easiest (SI 17). For this group, hole 2 is only the fourth hardest at 6.9, while hole 4 is third at 7.4.

Putting

Putting is not where most rounds are won

Putts ranged from 11 to 20 per round, while totals ranged from 28 to 51 in the same sessions. Tobias (28) and Laurits (29) reached the green in 15 and 17 strokes, and that is where their low rounds came from. Session 7 was the exception: Mikkel beat Laurits on the greens alone. Lukas's 38 in session 3 had 11 putts, the fewest recorded.

Session 7 was a three-putt day

Session 7 had 17 holes with three or more putts out of 24, including a five-putt for Laurits on hole 4. Over all counted rounds, Daniel has the most three-putts or worse with 12. No one has yet reached a green in one; Laurits has done it in two on seven holes, more than anyone else.

Source: data/sessions.csv, from the scorecards for sessions 1 to 5 and the golf app for sessions 6 and 7; 29 rounds in total. Putts were counted in sessions 2, 3, 6 and 7.
