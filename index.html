Session report

Golfparken Aalborg Sessions

Six sessions on a six-hole loop, played by a rotating group of six. Lower is better throughout. Par is 3 per hole, 18 for the loop, and every hole is capped at 9 (triple par). Session 1 was played before the cap, so its scores are capped here to match. The latest session was played on 18 October 2026.

Observations

What stands out

Results

Laurits has won four in a row

Laurits has won every session from 3 to 6 and has the best average by far at 37.0, seven strokes ahead of the next player. They won session 6 with 38 despite 18 putts, the most in any counted round. Their 35 in session 5 holds the only two pars in the data.

Laurits is unbeaten except once

Laurits is 4-0 with a tie against Lukas, 3-0 with a tie against Mikkel and 4-1 against Daniel. The only loss is to Daniel in session 1. Behind Laurits it is tight: Lukas leads Daniel 3-2 with a tie and Mikkel 3-1 with a tie, while Daniel and Mikkel are level at 2-2 with a tie.

Second place is a three-way race

Lukas (44.0), Daniel (44.5) and Mikkel (44.6) are separated by 0.6 strokes on average. One good round from any of them changes the order.

Most sessions are not close

Only session 1 (Daniel by 3) and session 4 (Laurits by 2) were decided by fewer than 8 strokes. The other four winners won by 8 to 15.

Scores have crept up since session 3

The field averaged 43.3, 40.5 and 38.8 in the first three sessions, then 43.8, 44.8 and 45.0. Sessions 2 and 3 were helped by the single low rounds from Tobias (28) and Laurits (29).

Session 6 at a glance

Three players on 18 October: Laurits 38, Daniel 46 and Lukas 51. Laurits won despite the most putts, because they reached the green in 20 strokes against 32 for Daniel and 36 for Lukas.

Players

Lukas and Daniel have played every session

Both were at all six. Lukas has scored 43 or 44 in four of them, with a best of 38 in session 3 and 51 in session 6. Daniel ranges from 41 to 48, and the 41 won session 1 once it was capped.

Daniel and Mikkel are the steadiest

Daniel's totals vary least (spread of 2.1 strokes), with Mikkel close behind (2.2). Lukas is at 3.8, mostly because of the 51 in session 6. Laurits varies most (4.9) because of 44 in session 1, and has not scored above 39 since.

Each player's best hole

Hole 5 suits Laurits best at 4.6, the lowest any regular averages on a hole. Daniel is best on hole 3 (5.7). Mikkel's best hole is hole 2 (6.2), and Lukas's is hole 4 (6.8).

Daniel and Mikkel fade on holes 4 to 6

Daniel averages 21.2 on holes 1 to 3 and 23.3 on holes 4 to 6; Mikkel goes from 21.4 to 23.2. Laurits gets better, from 19.0 to 18.0. Mikkel has taken the maximum 9 on hole 4 in all five rounds, and Daniel has taken 9 on hole 6 in five of six.

Holes

Holes 6 and 1 are the hardest

Hole 6 (7.9) and hole 1 (7.8) are almost level as the hardest holes. Hole 5 is the easiest at 6.2. About one hole in three ends on the maximum of 9 (49 of 150).

The course rating does not match this group

The club rates hole 2 hardest (SI 1) and hole 4 easiest (SI 17). For this group, hole 2 is only the fourth hardest at 6.8, while hole 4 is third at 7.6.

Putting

Putting is not where rounds are won

Putts ranged from 11 to 18 per round, while totals ranged from 28 to 51 in the same sessions. Laurits (29) and Tobias (28) reached the green in 17 and 15 strokes, and that is where their low rounds came from. Lukas's 38 in session 3 had 11 putts, the fewest recorded.

Multi-putts in session 6

Session 6 had 8 holes with three or more putts, including a five-putt each for Lukas (hole 3) and Laurits (hole 2). Over all counted rounds, Daniel has the most three-putts or worse with 8. No one has yet reached a green in one; Laurits has done it in two on five holes.

Source: golf_sessions_data.csv for sessions 1 to 5, and the app scorecards for session 6; 25 rounds in total. Putts were counted in sessions 2, 3 and 6. The file has putt rows for session 1, but they are not real counts and are left out. Six session 1 hole scores above 9 (10 to 15) are capped at 9, which lowers that session's totals: Lukas 49 to 44, Daniel 47 to 41, Laurits 51 to 44 and Mikkel 46 to 44. Only session 6 has a date, so sessions are shown in numeric order. Tobias played once, so their single round is shown but not ranked with the regulars.
